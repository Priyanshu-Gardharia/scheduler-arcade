ALGORITHM 01 • NON-PREEMPTIVE O(1) DISPATCH

THE QUEUE TITAN

FCFS
First-Come, First-Served

Scheduling Rule: Executes processes in strict order of their arrival. Once awarded CPU control, the titan never yields until execution terminates.

⚠ Convoy Effect: A massive job traps nimble short processes behind it.
ALGORITHM 02 • NON-PREEMPTIVE BURST-MINIMAL

THE SPEED DEMON

SJF
Shortest Job First (Non-Preemptive)

Scheduling Rule: Dispatches the ready process with the smallest CPU burst duration. In this non-preemptive scheduling, once a process seizes the CPU, it runs uninterrupted to completion even if a shorter process arrives.

⚠ Starvation Risk: Continuous streams of short bursts starve lengthy background computations indefinitely.
ALGORITHM 03 • PREEMPTIVE ROTATION CONFIGURABLE (q)

THE TIME WIZARD

RR
Round Robin Scheduling

Scheduling Rule: Allocates a configurable time quantum (q) to each ready process in circular order. Preempts immediately when the quantum timer fires, rotating the process to the queue tail.

⚠ Thrashing Hazard: A tiny time quantum causes crushing context-switching overhead, while too large degenerates into FCFS.
ALGORITHM 04 • NON-PREEMPTIVE RANK RANK-DRIVEN

THE PRIORITY KING

PRIORITY
Priority Scheduling (Non-Preemptive)

Scheduling Rule: Dispatches the ready process with the highest priority rank. A smaller priority number indicates a higher priority (e.g., Priority 0 is highest). Our initial implementation is non-preemptive: once a process is granted the CPU, it runs to completion without interruption.

⚠ Indefinite Blocking: Low-priority jobs (larger priority numbers) starve unless an aging protocol is enforced.
